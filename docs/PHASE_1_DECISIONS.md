## Version 1 exclusions

Virtual Advocate PI version 1 will not include features that could make the product appear to provide legal advice, medical advice, a DVA decision, a compensation estimate, or an automated entitlement assessment.

The following features are excluded from version 1:

1. No GARP M impairment points calculator
2. No compensation estimate
3. No DVA claim success prediction
4. No legal advice
5. No medical advice
6. No automated medical opinions
7. No direct DVA lodgement
8. No doctor portal accounts
9. No advocate marketplace
10. No payment or subscription system
11. No full case-management system
12. No automated claim strategy recommendations

These exclusions keep version 1 focused on evidence preparation, document preparation, doctor appointment preparation, evidence gap tracking, and draft claim-support documents for review.

## Supported post-2026 claim scenarios

Virtual Advocate PI version 1 will support five post-1 July 2026 claim preparation scenarios.

### 1. New permanent impairment claim for a new condition

Used when the veteran is preparing evidence for a condition that has not previously been accepted or compensated by DVA.

### 2. Worsening claim for an existing accepted condition

Used when the veteran has an already accepted condition and wants to organise evidence showing that the condition has worsened.

### 3. New condition with existing compensated conditions

Used when the veteran is preparing evidence for a new condition but also has previous DVA accepted or compensated conditions that may be relevant to the overall claim context.

### 4. Evidence pack for doctor or advocate review

Used when the veteran is not yet ready to prepare a claim pack but wants to organise evidence, symptoms, treatment history, and questions for a doctor, advocate, or support person.

### 5. Unsure / guided pathway

Used when the veteran does not know which preparation pathway applies. The app will ask plain-English questions and help organise the information without giving legal, medical, or DVA decision advice.

## First user journey

The first user journey for Virtual Advocate PI version 1 is the post-1 July 2026 PI Claim Starter Pack workflow.

The purpose of this journey is to help a veteran create a structured claim preparation workspace, enter condition and evidence information, identify evidence gaps, prepare questions for a doctor or advocate, review AI-assisted draft wording, and generate a DOCX/PDF claim starter pack.

### Journey overview

The user opens Virtual Advocate PI and signs in.

The user sees a clear disclaimer explaining that the app helps organise information and prepare draft documents, but does not provide legal advice, medical advice, financial advice, a DVA decision, a compensation estimate, or a guarantee of claim success.

The user creates a new claim workspace.

The user selects what they are preparing from the post-2026 pathway selector.

The app creates the workspace using the improved MRCA post-2026 framework.

The user enters condition details, including the condition name, diagnosis status, symptoms, treatment, medication, functional impact, lifestyle impact, work impact, and whether the condition is new or worsening.

If relevant, the user enters existing accepted condition history, including whether the condition was previously accepted by DVA, whether it was under VEA, DRCA, MRCA, or unknown, and whether previous PI, DCP, or other compensation evidence exists.

The user uploads evidence such as DVA letters, previous assessment letters, medical reports, specialist letters, imaging reports, medication lists, treatment summaries, service documents, or personal notes.

The app shows an evidence checklist and evidence gap tracker.

The app uses AI, where appropriate, to draft a veteran statement, worsening summary, doctor appointment questions, doctor request letter, and evidence gap summary.

The user reviews, edits, accepts, or rejects each AI-generated draft.

Only user-confirmed information and user-approved AI drafts are used in the generated documents.

The user generates a Post-2026 PI Claim Starter Pack.

The user downloads the pack as DOCX and/or PDF.

The user may take the pack to a doctor, advocate, lawyer, support person, or use it as a personal preparation document.

### First screen after login

The first major workflow screen after login will ask:

"What are you preparing?"

The available choices will be:

1. A new permanent impairment claim for a new condition
2. A worsening claim for an existing accepted condition
3. A new condition where you also have existing compensated conditions
4. An evidence pack for a doctor or advocate
5. I am unsure

### System behaviour

When the user creates a workspace, the app will set:

claim_framework = IMPROVED_MRCA_POST_2026

The app will then set one of the following claim scenario values:

NEW_CONDITION

WORSENING_EXISTING_CONDITION

NEW_PLUS_EXISTING

EVIDENCE_PREP_ONLY

UNSURE

### Minimum successful journey

The minimum successful version of this journey is complete when a user can:

Create an account or sign in.

Create a new claim workspace.

Select a post-2026 claim preparation scenario.

Enter one condition.

Enter basic symptoms, treatment, and functional impact information.

Upload or list at least one evidence item.

View an evidence checklist.

View evidence gaps.

Generate at least one AI-assisted draft for review.

Approve or edit the draft.

Generate a Post-2026 PI Claim Starter Pack as DOCX or PDF.

### Journey boundaries

The journey must not calculate GARP M impairment points.

The journey must not estimate compensation.

The journey must not predict whether DVA will accept or reject a claim.

The journey must not provide legal advice.

The journey must not provide medical advice.

The journey must not tell a doctor what clinical opinion to give.

The journey must not lodge anything directly with DVA in version 1.

## Review purpose

The purpose of this review is to confirm that Virtual Advocate PI version 1 has a clear, safe, buildable product scope before technical setup begins.

The review confirms that the app is being built as a post-1 July 2026 improved MRCA / VETS Act-first PI Claim Starter Pack platform.

The app will help Australian veterans organise evidence, prepare condition information, identify evidence gaps, prepare questions for doctors or advocates, review AI-assisted draft wording, and generate draft claim-support documents for review.

The app will not calculate GARP M impairment points, estimate compensation, predict DVA outcomes, provide legal advice, provide medical advice, or lodge claims directly with DVA.

## Confirmed version 1 product direction

Virtual Advocate PI version 1 will be built as a post-1 July 2026 improved MRCA / VETS Act-first PI Claim Starter Pack platform.

The version 1 product will focus on evidence preparation, condition intake, accepted-condition history capture, evidence gap tracking, AI-assisted draft review, doctor preparation questions, and DOCX/PDF document generation.

The product will be GARP M-aware, but it will not be a GARP M calculator.

## Confirmed safety position

All AI-generated content must be treated as draft wording only.

The user must review, edit, approve, or reject AI-generated drafts before they are included in any generated document.

The app must not invent symptoms, diagnoses, service history, medical conclusions, legal arguments, or DVA outcomes.

The app must not tell a doctor what opinion to give.

The app must not state that a claim will succeed or fail.
