# VETS Act and single Act reform reference

## Approved use

This reference may be used for general context about the post-1 July 2026 reform environment.

## Summary for RAG use

DVA states that the Veterans' Entitlements, Treatment and Support (Simplification and Harmonisation) Act 2025 was passed on 13 February 2025.

DVA states that from 1 July 2026, all new compensation and rehabilitation claims will be determined under a single Act, the improved MRCA.

DVA states that claims received before 1 July 2026 will be determined under the current tri-Act system.

## Safe drafting use

Use this only as general preparation context.

Do not provide legal advice.

Do not tell a veteran which Act will definitely apply without checking claim date and DVA context.

Do not predict DVA decisions.
