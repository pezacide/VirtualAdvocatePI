# GARP M approved guide reference

## Approved use

This reference may be used to support plain-English preparation prompts about GARP M-aware information gathering.

It may help structure questions about medical impairment, functional loss and lifestyle impact.

## Summary for RAG use

GARP M is an approved guide used in the MRCA permanent impairment context.

It includes instructions and tables for assessing medical impairment from accepted conditions.

Medical impairment includes physical loss or disturbance to a body part or system and the functional loss that results.

The app must not calculate impairment points or tell a veteran what GARP M outcome they will receive.

## Safe drafting use

Ask the veteran to describe symptoms, functional impact, treatment, medication effects, stability, flare-ups and daily life impacts in plain English.

Do not present the output as a formal impairment assessment.

Do not calculate points.

Do not diagnose.

Do not predict DVA outcomes.
