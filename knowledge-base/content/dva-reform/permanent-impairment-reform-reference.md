# Permanent impairment reform reference

## Approved use

This reference may be used for general preparation context about permanent impairment reform and post-1 July 2026 claim preparation.

## Summary for RAG use

DVA states that from 1 July 2026 all new permanent impairment claims will be determined under the improved MRCA.

DVA states that GARP M will be the only tool used for permanent impairment claims from 1 July 2026.

DVA states that a new GARP M chapter is planned to account for impairment from previously compensated claims.

## Safe drafting use

Use this to explain why historical DVA acceptance, previous PI or DCP assessment material, worsening information and current clinical information may be useful to organise.

Do not state eligibility.

Do not estimate points.

Do not calculate compensation.

Do not guarantee a result.
