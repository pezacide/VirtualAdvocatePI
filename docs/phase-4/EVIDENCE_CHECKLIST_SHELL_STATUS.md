## Current behaviour

The page displays grouped evidence checklist sections.

The checklist includes DVA history, medical diagnosis and treatment, functional impact, service connection notes, and appointment preparation.

The checkboxes are local UI only.

No backend evidence records are created yet.

## Safety note

The evidence checklist is preparation support only.

It does not tell the user what DVA will require, provide legal advice, provide medical advice, estimate compensation, or guarantee claim success.
