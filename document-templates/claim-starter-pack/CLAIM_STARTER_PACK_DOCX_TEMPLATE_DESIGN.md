# Claim Starter Pack DOCX Template Design

## Template name

Claim Starter Pack DOCX Template v1

## Purpose

This template defines the first DOCX structure for reviewed claim preparation material.

## Document title

Virtual Advocate PI - Claim Starter Pack

## Required disclaimer

This document is preparation support only. It does not provide legal advice, medical advice or a DVA decision. It does not submit anything to DVA, calculate impairment points, estimate compensation or guarantee a claim outcome. Please review all content before using it.

## Section 1 - Cover page

Workspace title.

Claim framework.

Claim scenario.

Generated date.

Preparation-only disclaimer.

## Section 2 - Workspace summary

Plain-English workspace overview.

Included active conditions.

Excluded or archived content note.

## Section 3 - Conditions included

Condition name.

Diagnosis status.

Primary or secondary condition marker.

Current summary fields where available.

## Section 4 - Accepted-condition history

Previous DVA acceptance details where recorded.

Original Act where recorded.

Previous compensation or assessment details where recorded.

Worsening claimed where recorded.

## Section 5 - Approved AI draft content

Only include AI drafts with ReviewStatus APPROVED.

Show draft type.

Show reviewed text if available, otherwise draft text.

Show source references where available.

## Section 6 - Evidence list

Evidence type.

Evidence status.

File name.

Provider or source.

Document date.

Upload status.

User notes.

## Section 7 - Evidence gaps and follow-up

Gap type.

Gap status.

Severity.

Plain-English explanation.

Suggested next step.

## Section 8 - GARP M-aware preparation summary

Include reviewed structured summary content only when available.

Do not calculate impairment points.

Do not present GARP outcomes as official.

## Section 9 - Doctor appointment questions

Include approved doctor questions draft where available.

Otherwise include generic appointment prep prompts only if reviewed or template-approved.

## Section 10 - Follow-up checklist

Outstanding evidence to organise.

Appointments to book.

Questions to ask doctor, advocate, lawyer or support person.

Files to upload or review.

## Section 11 - Review and sign-off

User review reminder.

Date reviewed.

Notes.

## Reviewed-only inclusion rules

Only active workspace content should be included.

Only active conditions should be included.

Only active evidence items should be included.

Only approved AI drafts should be included.

Archived records must be excluded.

Deleted uploaded files must not be shown as downloadable uploaded files.
