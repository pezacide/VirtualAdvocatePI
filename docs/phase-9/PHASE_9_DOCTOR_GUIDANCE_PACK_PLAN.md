# Phase 9 Doctor Guidance Pack Plan

## App

Virtual Advocate PI

## Phase

Phase 9 - Doctor guidance pack

## Purpose

Phase 9 adds a doctor guidance pack workflow to help veterans prepare for medical appointments and organise practical questions for their doctor, specialist or treating provider.

The doctor guidance pack must be respectful, non-pressuring and preparation-focused.

## Phase 9 source-of-truth task list

Design doctor guidance pack template.

Build clinical question generation workflow.

Add doctor-specific disclaimer and review checklist.

Export and test doctor guidance pack.

Milestone: Phase 9 complete.

## Safety boundary

The doctor guidance pack is preparation support only.

It does not provide legal advice.

It does not provide medical advice.

It does not diagnose conditions.

It does not tell a doctor what opinion to provide.

It does not pressure a doctor to support a claim.

It does not make DVA decisions.

It does not calculate impairment points.

It does not estimate compensation.

It does not guarantee claim outcomes.

It does not submit anything to DVA.

## Doctor-specific safety rule

The pack may help the veteran explain symptoms, treatment, medication, side effects, daily impact, functional limitations, flare-ups, stability, change over time and evidence gaps.

The pack must ask respectful questions only.

The pack must not instruct the doctor to write a specific opinion.

The pack must not ask the doctor to make a DVA decision.

The pack must not imply that a medical report guarantees a claim result.

## Reviewed-only rule

Doctor guidance pack exports should use active workspace records only.

Approved AI drafts may be included only if they are relevant to the doctor guidance workflow.

Archived records must be excluded.

Rejected, unapproved or still-reviewing AI drafts must be excluded.

Evidence files that have had uploaded file content deleted must not be treated as uploaded files.

## Intended pack sections

Cover page.

Preparation-only doctor disclaimer.

Appointment purpose.

Condition summary.

Symptoms and current impact summary.

Treatment and medication notes.

Functional impact prompts.

Evidence and reports to discuss.

Evidence gaps and follow-up items.

Clinical questions for the doctor.

Documents to request.

Appointment notes page.

Veteran review checklist.

## Initial output format

DOCX first.

PDF companion can follow the Phase 8 document export pattern.

## Data sources

Active workspace metadata.

Active conditions.

Active accepted-condition history where relevant.

Active question responses.

Active evidence metadata.

Active evidence gaps.

Approved doctor questions AI drafts where relevant.

Approved evidence gap AI drafts where relevant.

Approved doctor request letter AI drafts where relevant.
