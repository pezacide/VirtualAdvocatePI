# GARP M lifestyle and functional impact reference

## Approved use

This reference may be used to support plain-English questions and draft structure about daily life, work, domestic tasks, self-care, social impact, treatment and functional effects.

## Summary for RAG use

DVA explains that accepted conditions are considered together for permanent impairment, and lifestyle effects are relevant to permanent impairment compensation under MRCA.

A DVA claims assessor may use medical-practitioner information and lifestyle-rating information when calculating impairment and lifestyle effects.

The app must not calculate or estimate the veteran's impairment, lifestyle rating, compensation, acceptance or entitlement.

## Safe drafting use

Use this source to ask for clear examples of how a condition affects ordinary daily activities.

Use veteran-friendly wording.

Do not overstate certainty.

Do not replace medical evidence or DVA assessment.
