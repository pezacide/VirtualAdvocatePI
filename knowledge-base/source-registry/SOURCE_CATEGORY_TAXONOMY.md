# Source Category Taxonomy

## Purpose

This document defines the controlled source categories for the Virtual Advocate PI AI/RAG knowledge base.

Future source registry entries should use one of these categories so retrieval, prompt-building, citations and safety controls remain consistent.

## Safety rule

Only approved, active and non-archived source registry entries should be available for retrieval or AI prompt-building.

Draft, inactive, archived, unknown or unapproved sources must not be used for AI draft generation.

## Categories

### GARP_M_GUIDANCE

Approved guidance or internal explanatory material related to GARP M-aware preparation, lifestyle impact prompts and functional impact drafting.

Must not calculate impairment points or present GARP outcomes as official.

### DVA_REFORM_REFERENCE

Approved references about DVA reform, PI claim preparation context and post-reform workflow considerations.

Must not provide legal advice or predict DVA decisions.

### DVA_PROCESS_REFERENCE

Approved references explaining general DVA process concepts, evidence organisation and preparation steps.

Must remain general and must not claim that a particular action guarantees success.

### INTERNAL_TEMPLATE

Approved internal templates used to structure veteran statements, worsening summaries, doctor letters and claim pack cover notes.

Templates must keep user-review wording and preparation-only disclaimers.

### SAFETY_GUARDRAIL

Approved safety wording, refusal boundaries and prompt constraints for AI-assisted drafting.

Safety guardrails should be included in prompt-building for all AI draft generation tasks.

### EVIDENCE_PROMPT

Approved evidence collection prompts, evidence gap wording and plain-English evidence organisation guidance.

Must not state that evidence is legally or medically sufficient.

### DOCTOR_QUESTION_TEMPLATE

Approved question templates for helping a veteran prepare for a doctor, specialist or support appointment.

Must not instruct a doctor what opinion to provide and must not pressure for a diagnosis.

### CLAIM_PACK_TEMPLATE

Approved structure and wording templates for generated preparation packs and cover notes.

Must clearly label outputs as preparation support only.

### VETERAN_STATEMENT_TEMPLATE

Approved structure and wording templates for plain-English veteran statements.

Must preserve user voice and avoid exaggeration, certainty or legal conclusions.

### WORSENING_SUMMARY_TEMPLATE

Approved templates for explaining change over time, current impact and evidence gaps in worsening-related preparation.

Must not assert medical causation or formal worsening unless the user has provided supporting details.
