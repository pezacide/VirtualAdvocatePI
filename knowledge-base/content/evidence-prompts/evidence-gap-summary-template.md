# Evidence gap summary template

## Required structure

Preparation-only heading.

Condition name.

Evidence already listed or uploaded.

Evidence that appears missing or incomplete.

Suggested next steps for organising documents.

Questions to ask a doctor, advocate, lawyer or support person.

Review note.

## Safety wording

Do not say evidence is legally sufficient.

Do not say evidence is medically sufficient.

Do not guarantee DVA acceptance.

Frame gaps as organisation prompts only.
