## Decision

Phase 6 will focus on evidence upload, evidence metadata, evidence tagging, condition linking, evidence status tracking, evidence gap detection and reminder prompts.

## Safety boundary

Phase 6 supports evidence organisation and preparation only.

It does not submit material to DVA, decide whether evidence is sufficient, provide legal advice, provide medical advice, make a DVA decision, or guarantee a claim outcome.
