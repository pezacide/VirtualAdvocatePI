# Doctor appointment questions template

## Approved question areas

Can the current diagnosis or clinical picture be documented?

Can current symptoms and functional limitations be described?

Can treatment, medication and side effects be summarised?

Can stability, flare-ups or change over time be noted?

Is there any relevant specialist referral or investigation?

Are there documents the veteran should request or organise?

## Safety wording

These questions help prepare for a medical appointment.

They do not tell the doctor what opinion to give.

They do not pressure the doctor to provide a diagnosis.

They do not replace medical advice.
