# Phase 6 Evidence Upload and Gap Tracker Scope and Safety Boundary

## App

Virtual Advocate PI

## Phase

Phase 6 - Evidence upload and gap tracker

## Purpose

Phase 6 will improve the evidence workflow for claim preparation workspaces.

The phase will focus on evidence upload, evidence metadata, evidence tagging, condition linking, evidence status tracking, evidence gap detection and reminder prompts.

## Product boundary

Phase 6 supports evidence organisation and preparation only.

It must not submit evidence to DVA.

It must not tell the user that evidence is sufficient for DVA.

It must not guarantee a claim outcome.

It must not provide legal advice.

It must not provide medical advice.

It must not diagnose a condition.

It must not calculate compensation.

It must not calculate GARP M impairment points.

## Allowed behaviour

The app may help users upload or prepare evidence files.

The app may help users record evidence metadata.

The app may help users link evidence to claim workspaces and conditions.

The app may help users tag evidence by category, source and status.

The app may show evidence gaps based on missing user-provided information.

The app may suggest practical reminder prompts.

The app may show whether evidence has been listed, uploaded, reviewed or marked as ready by the user.

## Disallowed behaviour

The app must not say that DVA will accept evidence.

The app must not say that evidence proves service connection.

The app must not say that evidence proves impairment.

The app must not replace a doctor, advocate, lawyer or DVA decision-maker.

The app must not hide uncertainty.

Add Phase 6 documentation.

## Required safety wording

Recommended wording for Phase 6 evidence screens:

Preparation support only. This tool helps organise evidence and identify possible gaps. It does not submit material to DVA, decide whether evidence is sufficient, provide legal advice, provide medical advice, make a DVA decision, or guarantee a claim outcome.

## Success criteria

Phase 6 is complete when users can upload or prepare evidence, add metadata, link evidence to conditions, track evidence status, view evidence gaps, follow reminder prompts and use a smoke-tested evidence workflow.
