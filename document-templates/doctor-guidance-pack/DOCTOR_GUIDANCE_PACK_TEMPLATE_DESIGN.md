# Doctor Guidance Pack Template Design

## Template name

Doctor Guidance Pack Template v1

## Purpose

This template defines the first doctor guidance pack structure for helping a veteran prepare for a medical appointment.

The pack is not a medical instruction document. It is an appointment preparation document.

## Document title

Virtual Advocate PI - Doctor Guidance Pack

## Required disclaimer

This document is preparation support only. It does not provide legal advice, medical advice or a DVA decision. It does not tell a doctor what opinion to provide and does not pressure a doctor to support a claim. Please review all content before using it.

## Section 1 - Cover page

Workspace title.

Condition or conditions being discussed.

Appointment date if provided.

Doctor or clinic name if provided.

Generated date.

Preparation-only disclaimer.

## Section 2 - Appointment purpose

Plain-English reason for the appointment.

Examples include reviewing symptoms, documenting current treatment, discussing medication side effects, discussing functional impact, requesting copies of reports, or clarifying evidence gaps.

## Section 3 - Condition summary

Condition name.

Diagnosis status.

Primary or secondary condition marker.

Relevant current summaries where available.

Previous DVA acceptance context only where recorded and relevant.

## Section 4 - Symptoms and current impact

Plain-English symptom summary where available.

Current severity or frequency where recorded.

Flare-ups or triggers where recorded.

Stability or change over time where recorded.

## Section 5 - Treatment and medication notes

Current treatment summary.

Medication summary.

Side effects where recorded.

Specialist involvement where recorded.

Referrals, investigations or reports where recorded.

## Section 6 - Functional impact prompts

Daily routine.

Work or study.

Sleep.

Mobility.

Concentration.

Memory.

Self-care.

Domestic tasks.

Family and social life.

Flare-ups or bad days.

## Section 7 - Evidence and reports to discuss

Evidence type.

Evidence status.

File name.

Provider or source.

Document date.

Upload status.

Notes.

## Section 8 - Evidence gaps and follow-up items

Gap type.

Gap status.

Severity.

Plain-English explanation.

Suggested next step.

## Section 9 - Clinical questions for the doctor

Questions should be respectful and neutral.

Questions should help the veteran understand or document their current clinical picture.

Questions must not tell the doctor what opinion to provide.

Questions must not ask the doctor to make a DVA decision.

## Section 10 - Documents to request

Current clinical summary.

Relevant medical reports.

Specialist letters.

Medication list.

Treatment plan.

Referral letters.

Investigation results.

Functional impact notes where clinically appropriate.

## Section 11 - Appointment notes

Doctor comments.

Follow-up actions.

Documents requested.

Referrals discussed.

Next appointment.

## Section 12 - Veteran review checklist

Confirm the pack is accurate.

Confirm the pack is in plain language.

Confirm the questions are respectful.

Confirm the pack does not pressure the doctor.

Confirm no unapproved AI draft content is included.

Confirm archived records are excluded.

## Reviewed-only inclusion rules

Only active workspace content should be included.

Only active conditions should be included.

Only active evidence items should be included.

Only active evidence gaps should be included.

Only approved AI drafts should be included where relevant.

Archived records must be excluded.

Deleted uploaded files must not be shown as downloadable uploaded files.
